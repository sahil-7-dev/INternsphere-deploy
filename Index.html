QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, email us.

Is InternSphere a live product?

Not yet. InternSphere is currently a concept project — a fully functional prototype demonstrating what a modern, AI-assisted internship platform could look like. It isn't operating at a global or country-wide scale, and real companies and students aren't active on it today. The product vision, flows, and AI integrations are real; the marketplace around it is not (yet).

Is InternSphere free?

Yes — students apply and work for free, and companies post and review roles for free. No credit card required.

How do AI features work? Do you sell my data?

AI features are powered by Google Gemini. Your CV and submissions are sent to Gemini for analysis in real-time — we don't store them on third-party servers, and we don't sell data. Firebase Auth + Firestore rules keep your data scoped to you (and the company whose role you apply to).

What happens after I apply? Can I track the status?

Every application shows up on your dashboard with a live status — Pending, Shortlisted, Approved, or Rejected. Rejected applications can be re-applied. Approved applications unlock your Workroom with the tasks the company has assigned.

What is the "final task"?

Each internship has a task the company marks as final. Submitting it (and completing every earlier task) closes the internship out — you see a completion modal and a certificate follows within a week.

Can I edit my work with AI help?

Yes. Inside the Workroom, TARS can rewrite your draft — fix grammar, make it formal, improve wording, or draft a starter document based on the task brief. Every AI edit is reversible via the "Undo AI" button.

I'm a company — how do I rank 30+ applicants fast?

Open TARS on the company dashboard and pick "Find promising applicants". Choose a role — TARS reads every CV, ranks candidates by fit score with reasoning, and surfaces your top picks in seconds.

Do my drafts persist if I close the tab?

Yes. Every 2 seconds the Workroom saves your editor content locally, keyed to your task. Reopening the page drops you right back where you were — same task, same draft, same TARS conversation history.
