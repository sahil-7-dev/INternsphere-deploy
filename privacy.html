PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your data

Last updated: April 2026. InternSphere is currently a concept project — this policy outlines how data would be handled if the platform were in active use.

1. Information we collect

When you use InternSphere, we collect the information you provide directly — your name, email, profile details, CV, and any content you submit to internships or tasks. We also collect basic usage information such as which pages you view and actions you take to improve the experience.

2. How your information is used

Your data powers core features of the platform: matching you with relevant internships, generating AI-powered feedback on your CV and submissions, allowing companies to review your applications, and tracking your progress across roles. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. AI processing

AI features on InternSphere are powered by trusted providers such as Google Gemini. When you request AI assistance — resume analysis, CV feedback, or Q&A — the relevant content (your CV text, role description, or submitted task) is sent to the AI provider to generate a response. We do not retain these requests outside of what's needed to show you the result.

4. Data storage & security

Your account data is stored in Firebase, protected by Firebase Auth and Firestore security rules that scope access strictly to you and, where applicable, the company whose role you've applied to. Uploaded CVs are stored securely in Firebase Storage. While we take reasonable measures, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your rights

You can view, edit, or delete your profile information at any time from your dashboard. If you wish to remove your account entirely, contact us at InternSphere7@gmail.com and we'll assist promptly.

6. Cookies

InternSphere uses minimal cookies and local storage to remember your theme preference, keep you signed in, and preserve draft inputs. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, via email.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at InternSphere7@gmail.com.
